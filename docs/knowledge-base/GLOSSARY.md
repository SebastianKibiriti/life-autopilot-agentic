# Glossary

- **Agent event**: A structured record of an observation, decision, action, and outcome; not private chain-of-thought.
- **Arrival buffer**: Safety time reserved between expected arrival and commitment start.
- **Commitment**: A scheduled event the student intends to attend.
- **Departure planner**: Deterministic service calculating preparation and leave thresholds.
- **Preparation profile**: Explainable estimate of how long a student typically needs before leaving.
- **Replan**: Fresh evaluation because the current plan may no longer meet the commitment.
- **Taskmaster**: The hackathon track focused on event-driven workflows with autonomous routing.
- **Travel estimate**: Route duration and distance from current location to commitment destination.
- **Provider fallback**: A clearly labelled alternative used when a live external service fails.
